## 原话

「Q9 a，Q10 a」

## 落点

#1417 follow-ups of Q-3 a. Q-9 (a): an employer-change SEPARATE gets a system-created separation request of kind EMPLOYER_CHANGE; migrated separations get one imported request per person; no manual HR request and no exemption. Q-10 (a): when the only HR of an entity files on someone's behalf, any other user in the group holding the approval permission (e.g. parent-entity HR or the department head) may approve; filer and approver stay distinct, no self-approval, no mandatory per-entity second approver.
