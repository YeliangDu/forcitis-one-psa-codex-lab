# organization.payroll (薪酬，止于过账) — sign-off spec (#1403)

- Card: #1403. Author: one-design, 2026-11-05. Base: origin/main.
- Status: proposal. Basis: ADR-0087 decisions 1 to 5 (adopted), ADR-0086 decision 5 (per relationship), ADR-0051 decision 3 (incentive merged into payroll), ADR-0079 sections 2, 3.5, 4 and 5 (payroll boundary, monthly payroll lifecycle, events), ADR-0016 (posting), `organization-leave.md` section 9a (the payroll seam), `accounting-spine.md` (PR #1407; posting facts, periods).
- Evidence marks: **verified** = read on origin/main today. **unverified** = from memory or waiting on evidence. **#1373** = legacy payroll distillation, open: the legacy payroll formulas, the manual steps and the U8C posting accounts are NOT known yet, so this spec names every value that depends on them as **open** and invents none.
- Rule from the user (2026-11-05): where an explicit regulation differs from company practice the user decides; otherwise follow the explicit regulation; where none is explicit follow the current legacy practice.

## 1. What this spec decides

Payroll up to posting, per ADR-0087 decision 3: a monthly payroll per **paying legal entity**, with social insurance and housing fund per **employment relationship**, individual income tax and filing through `platform.integration` ports, bonus input through an approved manual port, the per-entity monthly payables submission as an outbound port, and the **posting fact** written to `operations.facts` when a month is approved. Not here (ADR-0087): vouchers, closing, U8C push, any payables aggregate, performance and recruitment.

## 4. Aggregates

All keyed by `employment_relationship_id` [R] where per relationship, and by `paying_legal_entity_id` where per paying entity. Names registered in the glossary are used as registered (`PayrollSettlementLine`, `PayrollDisbursement`, `RegisterPayrollDisbursementResult`, `InitiatePayrollRedisbursement`, `PayrollDisbursementResultRegistered`, `PayrollRedisbursementInitiated`, `CalculateMonthlyPayroll`). Every other command, event and aggregate name below is **proposed** and is registered through the glossary process (one-glossary) before its slice; so are `EmploymentRelationshipStarted/Ended`, `MonthlyConfirmationLocked` and `LeaveCompTimeExpired`, which exist only in proposals today. No `backend/glossary/proposed` file is added by this spec.

### 4.1 Payroll rule version (AGG-119, `payroll_rule_version`)

Configuration aggregate, one per version, effective interval not overlapping (INV-139; a lookup that finds none fails with a named error). Content (**structure only; values open**): IIT rate table and the monthly basic deduction, social-insurance and housing-fund ratios per insurance kind and jurisdiction, contribution base lower and upper limits, rounding rule. Command `PublishPayrollRuleVersion` (→ `PayrollRuleVersionEffective`, analytical; PRC-180); drafter ≠ approver (a new rule for AGG-119, not covered by INV-131's list; finance-HR approver is the permission catalogue's call). Statutory sources to cite when values are filled (**unverified**, from memory): the IIT law and its implementing regulation (cumulative withholding), the social-insurance law and local base rules, the housing-fund regulation (5% to 12%). Values taken from statute are marked as such; where legacy practice differs from an explicit rule, section 9 P-1.

### 4.2 Employee compensation profile (AGG-93, `employee_compensation_profile`)

Append-only versions with effective intervals, per **employment relationship**; holds the agreed pay (the authoritative value; the labour contract refers to a profile version, ADR-0079 section 2), the **paying legal entity** (default the relationship's employer; an override is recorded here with a reason), and pending adjustments (with source and idempotency basis). Commands: `CreateCompensationProfile`, `AdjustCompensationProfile`, `TerminateCompensationProfile` (subscribes `EmploymentRelationshipEnded`; explicit rather than inferred from person state, S-32). Event `CompensationProfileChanged` (analytical). Component structure (which pay items exist: base, allowances, subsidies) is the shape of A8 `formmain_0858`/`0841`: **open** until #1373; modelled as profile lines keyed by registered component keys.

### 4.3 Social-insurance and housing-fund enrolment (AGG-74, `social_insurance_enrollment_and_housing_fund_contribution_record`)

Per employment relationship (ADR-0086 decision 5): insurance kinds, enrolment state including **"not enrolled here"**, and contribution base versions (annual adjustment makes a new version, S-32); a re-hire creates a new enrolment record. Commands `EnrolInsurance`, `StopInsurance`, `AdjustContributionBase` (→ `InsuranceEnrolled`/`InsuranceStopped`, analytical). Subscribes `EmploymentRelationshipStarted`/`Ended` (never infers from person state). Whether a second employer enrols the person is ADR-0086 B-C: **open with HR and legal** (section 9 P-5); until answered an enrolment exists only when HR records it.

### 4.4 Special-deduction declaration (AGG-120)

Annual declaration per person and tax year, made by the person (HR may enter on their behalf, source recorded). Contains beneficiary information including minors: that part is sensitive and may need its own table and access level (U-11). **This aggregate waits for U-11 and a legal confirmation**: no slice builds it until then, and settlements in the meantime compute without special deductions only if the user so decides (P-6). Event `SpecialDeductionUpdated` (analytical).

### 4.5 Bonus determination (AGG-76) and the manual port

Until performance (AGG-106) exists the input is a **manual, approved port** (ADR-0087 5.3): a `BonusInput` row per relationship and payroll month with amount, source `MANUAL_APPROVED`, the approval number (via `platform.approval`) and a reason key from a selection list; the aggregate `DetermineBonus` (→ `BonusDetermined`, analytical) references the input. The port's commands are `RecordApprovedBonusInput` (writes the `BonusInput` row: relationship, payroll month, amount, reason key, approval number) and `VoidBonusInput` (voids a row; rows are never edited or deleted), as signed in `docs/data/reviews/organization-payroll-py3.md`. When performance is built the same port reads AGG-106 and the aggregate does not change. The same pattern carries shareholder dividends into the withholding input (ADR-0087 5.4) until AGG-109 exists. Incentive-plan versions (AGG-75) are **not** in this spec: the issue's scope does not list them and no input needs them yet; their slice is opened when the user names the first incentive rule.

### 4.6 Monthly payroll settlement (AGG-73, `monthly_payroll_settlement_statement`)

One per paying legal entity and payroll month. Contains `payroll_settlement_line`s (per employment relationship), the rule version used, and the posting and payment markers.

- States: `DRAFT` → `CALCULATED` → `IN_REVIEW` → `APPROVED`; `IN_REVIEW` → `CALCULATED` when the approval is rejected or withdrawn (recalculation allowed); `CANCELLED` from `DRAFT`/`CALCULATED` only (C-1) and a cancelled settlement frees the (entity, month) key (partial unique index excluding `CANCELLED`). `APPROVED` is final for amounts. Posting follows approval: `RETRY_PENDING` is retried by a scheduled job until the period opens (never a rejection, INV-92); `REJECTED` is shown to payroll and finance and re-submitted by `ResubmitPayrollPosting` (new idempotency attempt only after the cause is fixed; the amounts never change).
- Commands (→ events; preconditions):
  - `CalculateMonthlyPayroll` (PRC-124; → internal; the comp-time-to-pay conversion input is PRC-178; note: `organization-leave.md` 9a cited PRC-178 for the calculation by mistake; corrected in #1415): **only when every in-service person of the paying entity has a `LOCKED` monthly confirmation** (leave 9a), otherwise the result is "pending lock" with the list of persons not locked; reads the effective rule version for the month (none → fail, INV-139), the compensation profile in force each day, the enrolments and bases, the bonus inputs, the special deductions, unpaid-leave and overtime lines, comp-time conversions and, for leavers, the unused-leave settlement (T-10, section 9 P-7). Idempotent: recalculation of a `DRAFT`/`CALCULATED` settlement replaces its lines.
  - `SubmitPayrollForReview` (→ internal): starts the approval (`platform.approval`).
  - `ApproveMonthlyPayroll` (→ `PayrollApproved`; INV-70: only statutory deductions, paid in legal tender): called by the approval result; **calls the facts API in process** once with the candidate (spine: posting is not an endpoint, G36; `sourceId` = `settlementId`) and records the returned outcome in `posting_status`; the payables submission is a separate outbound port call written through the outbox (at least once, consumer idempotent). The payables submission does not wait for posting.
  - `MarkPayrollPosted` (→ internal): set from `BusinessFactAttributed` by `sourceId`; the facts event carries ids only.
  - `RegisterPayrollDisbursementResult` / `InitiatePayrollRedisbursement` (→ `PayrollDisbursementResultRegistered`, `PayrollRedisbursementInitiated`; glossary terms exist): per person result from the payables port; a failure puts the person on the re-pay list and never rewrites the approved settlement.
- Events: `PayrollApproved` (payload: settlementId, paying legal entity, payroll month, totals only; consumers: filing, audit), `PayrollPaid` (analytical). Ids and totals only.
- Corrections after approval: adjustment items in the next month's settlement lines (C-8).

### 4.7 Withholding and filing (AGG-95, `payroll_statutory_filing_record`)

Per paying legal entity and tax period: merge dividends into the withholding (manual port until AGG-109), generate the filing, submit it (→ `WithholdingFilingSubmitted`, analytical), register payment, correct. Two `platform.integration` ports: withholding calculation/submission and filing (ADR-0087 5.4); **stub adapters** until the channels are ready (the adapters belong to the platform, the contract to payroll). Social-insurance and housing-fund filings are separate records per relationship's employer. Posting of the tax and insurance payment is a later fact type (AGG-95 command "submit posting candidate"): **not in this spec's slices**; it joins when the user confirms payments are booked from payroll (P-8).

## 5. Permissions and data scope

Proposed permission codes follow the catalogue shape; the exact entries are fixed per slice. Separation of duties: calculate, review, approve and publish-rule are different holders; an approver may not be the one who calculated or who entered a bonus input (**mine**, SoD; confirm with finance). Employees read only their own payslip. HR operators see pay only with a payroll role; finance does not read payroll (ADR-0079 section 2). Scope: every table carries the **paying** `legal_entity_id` or inherits it from its parent; reads are filtered by the caller's entity scope, fail-closed. Classification: pay amounts `CONFIDENTIAL`; special-deduction beneficiary data sensitive (U-11); `employee_id` personal.

## 6. Module edges

`organization.payroll → organization.workforce` (api: relationships, employer, paying entity; events), `→ organization.attendance` (api: standard hours), `→ organization.leave` (api: locked confirmation lines, unused leave; events: `MonthlyConfirmationLocked`, `LeaveCompTimeExpired`), `→ platform.approval`, `→ platform.integration` (ports), `→ operations.facts` (api: submit; event `BusinessFactAttributed`), `→ payables.payment` **not yet**: the outbound port interface lives in payroll and a stub adapter returns "not available" until payables exists (ADR-0087 decision 4). Forbidden: payroll writing fact tables directly; any BC other than payroll reading payroll tables; payroll reading leave tables (only the api).

## 7. RULE ids

Reused: INV-70 (statutory deductions only), INV-105 (lock; corrections next month), INV-139 (rule versions do not overlap), INV-92 to 95 (posting, via the spine). New, numbered in the SSOT rule register (#1415): P-R1 = INV-143, P-R2 = INV-144, P-R3 = INV-145, P-R4 = INV-146, P-R5 = INV-147. P-R1 a settlement for a paying entity and month is unique; P-R2 calculation requires every in-service person locked; P-R3 an approved settlement's amounts never change; P-R4 one balanced posting candidate per approved settlement; P-R5 a person's pay never appears in an event, a fact or a payables submission detail beyond the entity totals.

## 8a. Pay history import (PY10, #1493; user 2026-11-05: history is imported because the opening balances need it, back to 2020; the Excel source and the user-side handling are in the fragments below, the latter being lead's handling)

Sources of the decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-05-164121-one-lead-历史工资要导入-因为计算期初需要.md`, `…164200-one-lead-在-excel-表.md`, `…164212-one-lead-要追溯到-2020-年.md` [V]. U8C `wa_*` holds 0 rows (#1373 comment 6662171271 [V]), so the Excel sheets are the only source.

- **Personal-data rule (baseline 2).** The sheets are row-level personal data. They never enter a session or the repo. The import runs on the user's side (a loader the user runs against the target database with their own credentials); sessions see column layouts (headers) and aggregates only (row counts per year, sums per year and component, counts of unmatched persons). The loader's code is ordinary repo code; its input and its reject list are not.
- **Options for where history lives.** (A) Load into `payroll_settlements` and `payroll_settlement_lines` with a source marker: reuses the lines, but drags 70+ months through the AGG-73 state machine, the posting and payment statuses and the frozen-when-approved trigger, and those rows were never calculated here. (B) A separate table `payroll_history_lines` (recommended): one row per person per paying entity per pay month, amounts by component as the sheet gives them, no state machine, no posting, immutable after load, with its own purge under the ten-year rule (P-9). Why B: history is a record of what was paid, not a settlement; it keeps AGG-73 clean and lets the sheets' components differ from the rule-version components.
- **Shape (sketch; column names provisional, the PY10 data-design sign-off applies naming standard 2.4: amounts paired with a currency, class words registered).** `payroll_history_lines`: id, employee_id (the person; history can predate any employment relationship record), employment_relationship_id null (为空表示 the sheet row could not be tied to a relationship), paying_legal_entity_id, pay_month_start_date, tax_year, component_key, amount, import_batch_id, source_row_ref (a position in the sheet, not a value). Unique `uk_…_batch_row`. `payroll_history_batches`: id, imported_by_user_id (the user), imported_at, sheet_label, row_count, file hash (not its content; unique, so the same file cannot load twice), status. Cross-BC ids carry no foreign key; classification and retention as every per-person payroll table (P-9: `CONFIDENTIAL`, ten years after the last separation, payroll purge function). The data-design sign-off is written on the PY10 card, after the column layouts arrive.
- **Opening balance read (internal to payroll).** `PayrollHistory.yearToDate(employee, payingEntity, taxYear, throughMonth)` returns the cumulative income, deductions and tax already withheld that the cumulative method needs at the first live month. PY4 calls it only for the cutover year; later years come from payroll's own settlements. Whether the cumulative figures reset when the withholding entity changes is statute-dependent and **unverified**; the read is per paying entity so either answer fits.
- **Matching persons.** By the employee number of the source (section 8b: `员工编号`, equal to the A8 member code, which the evidence queries already use as the match key between the staff table and the legacy accounts [V: `docs/data/evidence/dba-handoff/hire-date-step1b4a.sql:5`]), matched to `employee_id` in the loader; unmatched rows go to a reject output and only the count comes back.
- **Why 2020.** The decision gives the depth, not the use of the early years. Opening balances need only the cutover tax year; years back to 2020 serve payslip history, audit and retention. The user decided the depth (fragment 164212); the early years are built the same way; the loader may load years independently, so a failed old year never blocks the cutover year.
- **Done-when (for the test engineer).** (1) A loader dry run on synthetic rows reports row counts and sums per year and component and writes nothing. (2) Re-running the same batch is refused (unique file hash on `payroll_history_batches`). (3) A row whose person is unmatched lands in the reject output and in no table. (4) `yearToDate` over synthetic history equals the hand-computed sum per entity and tax year. (5) No sheet content, name or amount of a real person appears in the repo, a test fixture or a log. (6) The purge function removes a separated person's history rows with the others.

## 8b. Where the history lives in A8 (found 2026-11-05 by the registered forensic entry, user answer to P-13 「表头去查a8」; names, types and aggregates only, register fragments `docs/ops/legacy-read/2026-11-05-*-one-design.md` from 20:24 to 20:47)

The history is in **Seeyon custom forms** (`CAP_FORM_DEFINITION`, 254 forms; the older `FORM_DEFINITION` holds 14 stock forms, none about pay).
- **The pay history is one table, not one per year or entity: form `工资条`, main table `formmain_0864`, 29,440 rows, one row per person per pay month per paying company**, created 2020 and kept to 2026-10. Per year (rows / distinct persons): 2020 4,707 / 539; 2021 5,560 / 636; 2022 4,892 / 512; 2023 4,171 / 457; 2024 3,857 / 380; 2025 3,374 / 370; 2026 (10 months) 2,879 / 361 (sum 29,440 [V]).

Also attendance fields `是否全勤`, `公假天数`, `病假天数`, `事假天数`, month `考勤月份`, and text `调整备注`. Cumulative **income** and **deductions** are not stored; they are the sums of the monthly rows within the tax year.
- **Fill per year** (rows with a value / rows): gross (`应发工资`) and net (`实发工资`) are filled on 98.7% of rows in 2020 (4,645 of 4,707) and on at least 99% in every later year (2021: 5,511 of 5,560); `月度工资` in 2020 only 1,614 of 4,707, from 2021 on at least 99%; cumulative tax 2020 4,267, 2021 5,512, 2022 4,881, 2023 4,143, 2024 3,526, 2025 3,057, 2026 2,566 — **the cumulative tax is missing on 9 to 11 percent of the rows in 2024 to 2026**, so the opening read must not rely on it alone.
- **Person identifier.** `员工编号` (text) is present on all but 14 rows (1 / 5 / 4 / 4 in 2020 to 2023); 29,420 of 29,440 rows (1,131 of 1,134 distinct numbers) equal an `ORG_MEMBER.CODE`. ## 11. Done-when (for the test engineer)

1. A settlement is unique per paying entity and month; a second create for the pair fails.
2. `CalculateMonthlyPayroll` returns "pending lock" with the unlocked persons while any in-service person of the entity lacks a `LOCKED` confirmation, and computes once all are locked.
3. With no effective rule version for the month the calculation fails with a named error (INV-139); two overlapping effective versions cannot be published.
4. A person with two relationships paid by two entities appears in each entity's settlement with only that relationship's lines; social insurance follows the relationship's employer and withholding follows the paying entity.
5. Approval calls the facts API exactly once with one balanced candidate (`sourceId` = settlement id) and creates one payables submission; replaying the approval result submits nothing new (idempotency key; `DUPLICATE` maps to `POSTED`); the candidate contains no employee id.
6a. A `RETRY_PERIOD_CLOSING` outcome sets `RETRY_PENDING` and writes no rejection; the job retries and reaches `POSTED` once the period opens. A `REJECTED(reason)` outcome sets `REJECTED` with the reason key, is visible to payroll and finance, and `ResubmitPayrollPosting` after the cause is fixed reaches `POSTED`.
6. `BusinessFactAttributed` with the settlement's `sourceId` sets `posting_status = POSTED`; a replayed event changes nothing; an event for an unknown source is ignored and logged.
7. An approved settlement's amounts cannot be updated or deleted at database level; a correction appears as an adjustment line in the next month's settlement, tagged with the original month.
8. A failed payment result puts the person on the re-pay list and leaves the approved settlement unchanged; a re-disbursement links to the failed one.
9. An employee reads only their own payslip; HR without a payroll role and finance cannot read any settlement line (403); a caller outside the entity scope gets 404-equivalent for another entity's settlement.
10. Events and the payables submission contain ids and entity totals only; no per-person amount appears (architecture/contract test over the event schemas).
11. The approver of a settlement cannot be the person who calculated it, and cannot approve a bonus input they entered (SoD).
12a. A rejected or withdrawn approval returns the settlement to `CALCULATED`; a cancelled settlement allows a new settlement for the same entity and month.
12b. A correction that reduces pay posts as the opposite `side` with a positive amount.
12. Deductions outside the statutory list are refused (INV-70).
13. Module edges: payroll does not read leave tables or write fact tables; no BC but payroll reads payroll tables (architecture tests).
