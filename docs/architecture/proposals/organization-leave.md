## 3. Options and choices (technical; mine to choose)

**3.1 How a balance is stored.**

| | A. Account row with counters (entitled, used, held) | B. Account plus append-only ledger, balance derived (chosen) |
|---|---|---|
| Audit of every change | needs a second history table | the ledger is the history |
| Idempotent re-delivery of an event or approval result | per-counter guards | unique (source kind, source id, line kind) per account |
| Sealed year never rewritten (ADR-0079:123) | counters can drift unnoticed | sealed year refuses new lines; corrections are new lines in the open year |
| Cost | cheap read | one sum per read; fine at this volume (one row per person x type x year) |

Chosen B. Reason: INV-68 and the sealed-year rule both want "append, never rewrite", and the ledger gives idempotence for free.

**3.2 Unit of quantity.** Each leave type declares a ledger unit: `MINUTE` (hour-granular types, comp time) or `DAY` (stored as hundredths of a day, integer). A `DAY` type is never converted to minutes inside the ledger, so a standard-hours change (entity A 450 minutes, entity B 480, a new AGG-108 version) cannot change the number of days a person is entitled to. Conversion happens once, per day of leave taken: the request's day row stores both the minutes (from that day's standard hours, INV-116, no default fallback; this is what the timesheet and payroll read) and the day fraction that is charged to the ledger (day minutes / that day's standard minutes). The conversion date is the day of leave taken. Rounding is T-3, applied when the day fraction is formed, never inside the ledger. Whether a type is `DAY` or `MINUTE` is part of the rule version (T-3).

**3.3 Who is the approver path of overtime.** ADR-0079 §5 invariant 9 (:125) puts leave and overtime through `platform.approval`; the closeout commands replace overtime approval by a punch check. See T-9. Technical choice made here: overtime goes through `platform.approval` as ADR-0079 says; the punch check is a submit-time validation (refuse, or flag, per T-9), not an approver.

**3.4 Rule version (AGG-118) belongs here.** The card lists five aggregates; the planned rows and the closeout list a sixth, AGG-118 (leave types, entitlement rules, carry-over rules, exempt threshold), assigned to B-27 (`planned.yaml:1121-1130`). Leave quota cannot exist without it, so this spec includes it as a version-table aggregate and lead ruled on 2026-11-05 to include it (Q-1, section 9).

**3.5 Time-dependent quota inputs (service years, employer entity, unpaid periods) [R].** Leave is counted **per employment relationship** (user ruling, #1295; ADR-0086 decision 5): every account, request and ledger line carries `employment_relationship_id`, never only the employee. Entitlement is computed by the account command from an as-of read of the workforce api `EmploymentRelationships` (`on(employee, date)`, `get(id)`, `employerOn(id, date)`), and the inputs used (relationship start date, cumulative service basis, relationship end date, unpaid periods, employer entity) are recorded in a child row of the GRANT or ADJUST line (`leave_grant_basis`), so a sealed year stays reproducible and no replica of workforce data is kept. Events only trigger a recompute, and they are the **relationship-level** events of ADR-0086 decision 6 (`EmploymentRelationshipStarted`, `...Ended`, `...PhaseChanged`, `...UnpaidLeaveChanged`, `...Corrected`), which cover every RULE-515 trigger; person-level `Employee*` events are not subscribed (ADR-0086 decision 6). `EmployeeLegalEntityChanged` is not a trigger: it moves the **attribution** (cost and data-scope) entity, which does not touch leave (ADR-0079 invariant 4, meaning 3). History-only corrections are published as `EmploymentRelationshipCorrected` once ADR-0086 is built. Gaps (each needs a card from lead): (a) the cumulative service basis (prior service across employers, `docs/data/employment-start-date-opinion.md`; statute counts different employers, 人社部令第1号 art. 3-5, quoted in `employment-relationship.md` section 8) must be exposed on `EmploymentRelationships.get`; (b) the unpaid-leave **end** is carried by `EmploymentRelationshipUnpaidLeaveChanged` (G5 needs the period end); (c) until ADR-0086 is adopted and built, leave cannot be implemented: slice R (section 10) blocks L3. The employer entity of a relationship is immutable (ADR-0086 decision 2) except by legal succession (`employerOn`); a rule version's scope is the **employer** entity, never the attribution entity. Alternative rejected: a local projection fed by events, which cannot see (a)-(c) and misses history-only corrections.

### 4.1 AGG-118 Leave rule version (configuration)

- Data: leave type code, applicability scope (employer entity (the relationship's, ADR-0086) or all; the source sets it by city and service years, city is dropped here until the user names a city rule, because INV-123's exclusion constraint needs one scope key), effective-from date, entitlement rule rows (service-year band, quantity in the type's ledger unit, proration mode), carry-over rule (cap in the ledger unit, expiry), request unit (minute / half-day / day), allow-negative flag, exempt-from-approval threshold (minutes) and proof-required flag per type, basis reference, publisher.
- State: one, `IN_FORCE`; a new version supersedes; never edited, corrections are new versions that do not re-apply to sealed years (D15).
- Command: `PublishLeaveRuleVersion` (permission `leave:rule:publish`). Event: `LeaveRuleVersionPublished`.
- Invariant INV-123: one version in force per (leave type, scope) on a date; enforced by exclusion constraint on non-voided rows, as W2 versioned tables.
- **The values inside the rule rows are business treatments (T-1..T-8).** The structure above is mine; the numbers are the user's (or, for T-1, T-3, T-5, T-7, T-8, T-10, the legacy system's result).

### 4.2 AGG-71 Leave balance account

- Two account kinds. Year accounts: one per (employment relationship, leave type, leave year) [R], for `DAY` types such as annual leave. Rolling accounts: one per (employment relationship, leave type) with no year and no seal, for comp time (and for carry-in if T-7 gives it an expiry): their grants are lots, each with its own expiry, so a 2026 grant can expire in 2027 without writing to a sealed year (closeout `commands.yaml:1701`, 补休额度行独立于年度). Created by `OpenLeaveYearAccount` (automatic on `EmploymentRelationshipStarted` for the start year of that relationship [R]; on year start for the rest; never implicitly by a hold). Requires the rule version in force on the open date, else it fails with an alert (no default).
- Ledger line kinds: `GRANT` (entitlement, proration, comp-time grant), `CARRY_IN`, `HOLD`, `RELEASE`, `USE`, `REVERSE_USE`, `ADJUST` (HR manual, with reason), `EXPIRE`, `CARRY_OUT`. Each line: quantity in the type's ledger unit (non-negative for every kind except `ADJUST`, which is signed; a CHECK per kind enforces the sign), source kind and id, effective date, expiry date (lots only), `consumes_line_id` (the grant line it draws from; mandatory on `USE`, `HOLD`, `RELEASE`, `EXPIRE`, `REVERSE_USE` of lot types), actor.
- Derived balance, per account for year accounts and per lot for rolling accounts: sum(GRANT, CARRY_IN, REVERSE_USE, ADJUST) - sum(HOLD net of RELEASE and USE, USE, EXPIRE, CARRY_OUT). A request that draws from several lots (earliest expiry first, RULE-516) writes one HOLD and one USE line per lot, choosing only lots that are valid on every leave day of the request, i.e. whose expiry date is not before the day (closeout `commands.yaml:1701`; a leave on the expiry day itself counts as valid). When a RELEASE or REVERSE_USE returns minutes to a lot whose expiry has already passed, the same transaction writes an EXPIRE line for them and publishes `LeaveCompTimeExpired`, so nothing returns to a dead lot and payroll is told (RULE-516). Carry-in without an expiry lives in the year account and is drawn before the year's own grant; carry-in with an expiry is a lot in the rolling account and is drawn by earliest expiry; which of the two is used is T-7. Lot expiry is taken from the rule version in force on the grant date and stored on the grant line. Whether the balance may go below zero is T-4.
- Carry-over is written **inside** `CloseLeaveYear` [M]: when the relationship continues into the next year, the same transaction writes `CARRY_OUT` on the closing account and `CARRY_IN` on the next one (cap per T-7; the next account is opened first if it does not exist). A separate carry-over command would let the seal strand the balance, because a sealed year takes no line (model gap G-4).
- Corrections that land on a sealed year [M]: every line that must reverse or adjust something of a sealed year (`ReverseLeaveUse` after the seal, the entitlement `ADJUST` of an event about that year) is written as an `ADJUST` line in the next open year, quoting the original line (RULE-514); if no year is open, the command waits and alerts, it never writes to the sealed year (model gap G-3). Open sub-case: a relationship that ended in the sealed year has no next year, so the wait never ends; the user decides in confirmation row T-13 (hand the returned days to payroll as unused leave, T-10). `AdjustLeaveBalance` by HR refuses a result below zero unless T-4 allows it (model gap G-5); an entitlement cut driven by an event (T-3, T-5) can also go below zero, which T-4 must answer.
- States (year accounts only): `OPEN` -> `SEALED` (terminal; ADR-0079:123, after `CloseLeaveYear`). A request spanning the year end holds its 2027 part on the 2027 account at submit, so no hold of the closing year is kept open by it; `CloseLeaveYear` still refuses while a hold of that year is active. Rolling accounts have no seal.
- Commands: `HoldLeaveBalance` (INV-68; refuses when it would exceed available unless T-4 allows), `ReleaseLeaveHold`, `ConsumeLeaveHold` (on approval), `ReverseLeaveUse` (on 销假/ cancel after start), `GrantCompTime`, `ExpireCompTime`, `AdjustLeaveBalance`, `CloseLeaveYear`. Retry-safe: unique (account, source kind, source id, line kind, consumes_line_id). [M] For a line driven by a relationship event the key is (relationship, leave type, source event id, original year, line kind) and does not contain the target account: a redelivery after the seal is redirected to the next year (RULE-514) and must hit the same key, otherwise it writes the entitlement change twice (model gap G-6).
- Consumes: `EmploymentRelationshipStarted`, `...Ended`, `...PhaseChanged`, `...UnpaidLeaveChanged`, `...Corrected` [R] (each triggers a recompute of entitlement as a new GRANT/ADJUST line with its basis row, 3.5; formulas per T-1..T-6).
- Publishes: `LeaveCompTimeExpired` (minutes, account, date) for the payroll seam; nothing else.

### 4.3 AGG-70 Leave request

- Data: request id, employment relationship (the one the leave is taken from; a person with one relationship in service gets it defaulted, with two the person chooses, never guessed [R]), employee (denormalised id for the data-scope check only), leave type, start and end instants, reason, proof attachment reference, minutes per day (split by each day's standard hours; non-workdays 0), balance hold id.
- States: `DRAFT`, `SUBMITTED` (auto-approve path only), `IN_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED` (terminal), `WITHDRAWN` (terminal, before start), `RECALLED` (销假, terminal), `CANCELLED` [M] (terminal; the system cancels a request on days the relationship is no longer in service), where "`APPROVED` stays `APPROVED` after it is fully taken".
- Commands: `SubmitLeave` (refused on a day the relationship is not in service; holds balance in the same transaction; routing by rule version: inside quota, rule-allowed, under the exempt threshold and no proof needed -> auto-approve, else `ApprovalProcesses.start`), `ApproveLeave` / `RejectLeave` (from `ApprovalCompleted`, like `ApplyApprovalResult`), `WithdrawLeave` (applicant only; in approval via `ApprovalProcesses.withdraw` and `ReleaseLeaveHold`; approved before start via `ReverseLeaveUse` [M]: the hold was already consumed, so a release writes nothing and the used minutes would stay used, model gap G-2), `RecallLeave` (销假: fact registration, not re-approved; reverses used minutes).
- Relationship events that cut service [M]: on `EmploymentRelationshipEnded` and on a `EmploymentRelationshipCorrected` that moves the start later, the leave side cancels every `IN_APPROVAL` or `APPROVED` request of that relationship that has a day outside the service period: it withdraws the approval process through the platform (idempotent), then releases the hold or reverses the use, and sets `CANCELLED`. A later `ApprovalCompleted` for a cancelled request is ignored. Releasing only the open holds on `Ended` (the earlier wording) leaves an approval that arrives afterwards unable to consume its hold, and leaves holds and approved leave on days outside service after a correction (model gaps G-1, G-1b).
- Events published: `LeaveApproved`, `LeaveRejected`, `LeaveWithdrawn`, `LeaveRecalled` (consumers: timesheet, payroll seam, reminders; names registered by the slice that implements them). Subscribed: `ApprovalCompleted`.
- Timesheet seam (RULE-398/404/405). Direction: `cost.timesheet -> organization.leave`, api and published events only, never tables. Surface: read API `LeaveHours.forDay(employee, date)` (approved and pending minutes, **one line per relationship** so the caller can see which entity's leave it is; whether timesheet sums them is T-11 [R]) and the events `LeaveApproved`, `LeaveRejected`, `LeaveWithdrawn`, `LeaveRecalled` (RULE-405 needs the rejection). This edge does not exist today and `TimesheetOvertimeSeamGuardTest` forbids it, so **a separate card (lead opens it, design rules the guard text) must add the edge to `module-dependencies.txt` and narrow the guard to allow exactly the `organization.leave.api` leave-hours type and the leave events**; until then no slice may call the API from timesheet. RULE-412 stands for overtime approvals and comp-time balances, which stay unreadable.

### 4.4 AGG-72 Overtime request

- States: `REQUESTED`, `IN_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, then `COMPENSATED_PAY` or `CREDITED_AS_COMP_TIME` (terminal), `REJECTED` (terminal), `WITHDRAWN` (terminal; the applicant withdraws only while `IN_APPROVAL`, via `WithdrawOvertime` and `ApprovalProcesses.withdraw`).
- Commands: `SubmitOvertime` (names the employment relationship it is worked for [R], same default rule as leave; validates against punches, T-9; refuses overlap with approved leave; compensation mode limited by day type: statutory holiday pay only), `ApproveOvertime` / `RejectOvertime` (from `ApprovalCompleted`), `MarkOvertimeCompensated`, `MarkOvertimeCreditedAsCompTime` (calls `GrantCompTime`).
- Events: `OvertimeApproved` (consumed by AGG-97 inside the module), `OvertimeCompensated`.
- RULE-412 stands: nothing outside this module reads overtime approvals or comp-time balance except through the read API.

### 4.5 AGG-69 Attendance record

- Append-only fact, state `RECORDED` (record kind punch / outfield / make-up is an attribute). Idempotent import on (source system, external record id).
- Commands: `ImportAttendanceRecords` (whole batch fails and alerts if a person cannot be resolved), `MakeUpPunch` (counts and window: business treatment U4, not decided).
- The attendance source system is undetermined (R2); this aggregate is the last slice and its import adapter is behind a port. Biometric and location columns follow ADR-0080 decision 2 (supersedes the ADR-0079 U-14 sentence): biometric punch needs separate consent and an alternative; field location needs a regulation and is limited to working time; sick leave stores only the proof and the period.

### 4.6 AGG-97 Monthly attendance confirmation

- Per employee x month: attendance days, leave by type, overtime by compensation mode, correction rows tagged with the original month.
- States: `GENERATED` -> `CONFIRMED` -> `LOCKED` (terminal). Missing import days are a failed precondition, never full attendance.
- Commands: `GenerateMonthlyConfirmation`, `RegenerateMonthlyConfirmation` (only `GENERATED`), `ConfirmByEmployee`, `ConfirmByDefault` (overdue or resigned), `LockMonthlyConfirmation`. Events: `MonthlyConfirmationConfirmed`, `MonthlyConfirmationLocked`.
- INV-105: after lock nothing changes; later changes land as correction rows of the next month. A leave `IN_APPROVAL` is not counted.

### 4.7 Inconsistencies in the sources, and what this spec does with them

1. The closeout `锁定` command carries the idempotency key and `used_by` of the year-end carry-over command (copy-paste). Spec uses the right keys: lock = (employee, month), carry-over = (employee, leave type, year).
2. `SUBMITTED` exists in the state machine but not in the aggregate draft; kept, as the auto-approve path needs it.
3. planned.yaml lists fewer commands than the closeout; the closeout set is used (reject, reverse used, comp-time grant and expiry, regenerate, default-confirm).
4. INV-116 (standard-hours version) is owned by attendance but cited by leave commands; leave only reads it through attendance's api.
5. ADR-0051:96 says attendance starts no approval; this context starts them, which is why leave left attendance. No cycle.

## 5. Permissions and data scope

## 6. RULE ids

New file `docs/business/rules/leave-rules.md`, ids RULE-509.. (bc tag `organization.leave`). Existing RULE-398..405, 412, 413 stay in the timesheet files; RULE-412/413 get the bc tag by cross-reference only.

| Id | Rule (summary) |
|---|---|
| RULE-509 | A leave request holds balance in the same transaction it is submitted; it is refused when the balance is not enough, unless the rule version allows (INV-68, T-4) |
| RULE-510 | Leave minutes are split by the standard hours of each day; a non-workday counts zero; a missing standard-hours version refuses the request |
| RULE-511 | A request inside quota, rule-allowed, under the exempt threshold and with no proof needed is approved without a person; otherwise it goes to approval |
| RULE-512 | A rejected or withdrawn request releases its hold; a recalled leave gives back used minutes; nothing is deleted |
| RULE-513 | A balance account is opened by the year and the rule version in force, never implicitly; a missing version fails and alerts |
| RULE-514 | A sealed leave year is never rewritten; corrections and carry-over are new lines in the open year |
| RULE-515 | Entitlement changes caused by hiring, separation, unpaid leave or legal-entity change are new ledger lines computed from the rule version; the formulas are the business treatments |
| RULE-516 | Comp time is granted per overtime record with its own expiry and used earliest-expiry first; expiry reports the minutes to payroll and pays nothing here |
| RULE-517 | Overtime is approved through the approval platform; statutory-holiday overtime can only be paid; overlap with approved leave is refused |
| RULE-518 | Monthly confirmation is generated only when every day of the month has an import batch; after lock nothing changes and later changes go to the next month marked with the original month |
| RULE-519 | Attendance records are append-only; a make-up punch is a separate record |
| RULE-520 | The leave read API is the only way other modules see leave hours, balances, overtime approvals or comp time |

Each rule gets the full block (Category, Priority, Source, Plain English, Specification, Tests, Confidence) in the rules file; sources: the cited ADR line and the closeout command. Ids may shift if another PR takes RULE-509.. first; the rules file is the register.

## 8. Data design (signed by design for this repo; the gate in `docs/data/data-design-gate.md` applies per schema PR)

Schema `organization_leave`. Naming per `docs/rules/naming-standard.md`: every table has a UUIDv7 `id` and references use `<object>_id` (business codes are unique constraints, never keys); audit columns `created_at`, `updated_at`, `version` (append-only tables carry only `created_at`, since they are never updated); no generic `created_by` / `updated_by` (state-change operators go to `state_transitions.actor_id`, other actions to audit; a column exists only for an operator with business meaning, named for the action); booleans start with `is_` / `has_`; no `jsonb` for business content (D18); no mutable history (W2 rule: versions append and void). Column lists below name the business columns only; the audit columns are implied.

| Table | Key columns | Notes |
