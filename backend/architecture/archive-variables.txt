# Variables of the process engine that an archive may carry (ProcessArchiveJob) and how they are classified. Everything else in ACT_HI_VARINST is left out:
# process variables hold form content. ProcessArchivePolicyTest keeps this list equal to the constants in the job.
# Format: <PROCESS|TASK> | <variable name, * at the end for a prefix> | <level> | <why it is safe>
PROCESS | contentDigest | INTERNAL | fingerprint of the content, no content itself
PROCESS | routed_* | INTERNAL | which routes the instance took (booleans derived from the form), no personal data
TASK | systemComplete | INTERNAL | the task was completed by the system, not by a person
TASK | advisory | INTERNAL | the task is advisory only
TASK | assignedAt | INTERNAL | when the task was assigned (epoch milliseconds)
