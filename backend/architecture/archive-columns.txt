# Columns of platform_audit.audit_entries and whether the audit archive carries them (AuditArchiveJob.COLUMNS). Every column of the table is listed;
# a column added to the table without a line here fails DataArchitectureTest, and a column classified RESTRICTED in data-classification.txt can never be ARCHIVE.
# AuditArchiveColumnsTest keeps the ARCHIVE lines equal to the job's list. An archived column must be a bigint, integer, boolean, text, uuid or timestamptz:
# the job refuses any other type instead of turning it into text.
# Format: <column> | <ARCHIVE|EXCLUDE> | <level> | <why>
id | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | the entry's number, the archive's progress marker
action | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | what was done
actor_id | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | who did it (an id, no name)
on_behalf_of_id | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | on whose behalf, for explicit proxy actions
subject_key | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | what it was done to ({type}:{id})
detail | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | one-line description without keys or personal data
event_id | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | the domain event that caused the entry, the idempotency key
correlation_id | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | ties the entry to the request or job run
change_reason | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | the reason the person gave for a change
before_text | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | the value before a change, as text without secrets (the entry refuses text that looks like one)
after_text | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | the value after a change, as text without secrets
created_at | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | when the audited fact happened
updated_at | EXCLUDE | INTERNAL | always equal to created_at (the row is never updated)
source_address | ARCHIVE | SENSITIVE | the client address of a login or session end, kept for tracing a security incident after the online period (user decision 2026-10-15); personal data, so the archive store is access-controlled like the audit trail
outcome_kind | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | whether the login or the session end succeeded or failed
login_method_kind | ARCHIVE | INTERNAL | which way the person logged in (WeCom, SSO, ticket, emergency)
